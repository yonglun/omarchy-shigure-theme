Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: the first original wallpaper concept for Shigure II / 月下, a Japanese ukiyo-e inspired DARK Linux desktop theme. Make one full-bleed horizontal 16:9 artwork, request 3840 x 2160.
Primary request: a serene moonlit coastal bay, designed with the unmistakable Edo-period MULTICOLOR WOODBLOCK PRINT visual grammar of Utagawa Hiroshige. This is a newly composed contemporary interpretation, not a copy or scan of an existing print.
Scene: a round pale warm-paper full moon in the upper-right quadrant above a quiet bay; very low distant indigo islands and headlands along the lower third, a slightly winding shoreline starting at far left and receding towards the middle distance. A small cropped cluster of exquisitely carved dark reeds rises from the bottom-right corner, confined to the outer edge. No boats, no people, no buildings.
Composition: panoramic and boldly asymmetrical. The center half of the image is restful, unoccupied dark blue sky and flat water, with very few widely spaced horizontal woodcut ripple lines. The moon is modest, about 5 percent of the image width, and sits well inside the top and right edges. Low islands provide a delicate rhythm across the horizon without forming realistic alpine mountains. The reeds and moon remain visible when desktop windows cover the center. Dark ink blue and slate blue occupy most of the area; warm paper is limited to the moon and a few restrained printed accents. No large pale sky band.
Style/medium: crisp fine sumi keyblock outlines, elegant carved contour rhythm, simplified flattened Japanese pictorial perspective, broad FLAT printed pigment shapes, confidently stylized vegetation. Delicate bokashi printing gradation only within the sky or water. Very faint fresh washi fiber texture, subtle hand-printed pigment irregularity, precise graceful linework. Every form must look designed as a woodblock print, with graphic ink planes rather than modeled volumes.
Color palette: deep ink indigo near #101F2B, raised blue near #1B3040, muted Prussian blue, slate/grey blue, tiny warm ivory moon near #F0E7D5, subdued pine tones in edge reeds. Restrained midtones so the picture is legible rather than crushed black. No golden glow.
Mood: still, cool, nocturnal, spacious, contemplative; printmaking, not photography.
Constraints: no text, no calligraphy, no title cartouche, no seals, no signatures, no logo, no watermark, no border. No stars, no glowing aura, no cinematic lighting, no photorealism, no 3D, no realistic volumetric clouds, no fantasy art, no anime painting, no airbrush, no realistic glittering moon reflection, no dense texture in the central space. Preserve authentic woodblock lines and flat color areas. One scene only.
